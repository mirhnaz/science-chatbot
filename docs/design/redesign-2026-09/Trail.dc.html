What is the nucleus of a comet made of?

Think of a giant, dirty snowball: mostly ice, mixed with dust and bits of rock. When the comet swings close to the Sun, that ice turns straight into gas and streams away as the glowing tail we see from Earth. A nucleus can be 40 km wide, bigger than some mountains!
